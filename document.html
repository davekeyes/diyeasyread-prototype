Dorothy's Story and Safety Questions

This is about Dorothy who is 93 years old.

She has dementia and lives alone.

Her family is worried about her safety.

1

Dorothy is 93 years old.

Her husband died 10 years ago.

She lives by herself in her own house.

She is proud to be independent.

💡 Independent means doing things for yourself without help.
2

She wants to keep living in her house.

She raised her family in this house for 50 years.

She loves gardening.

She loves her family.

3

She has 4 children.

She has 15 grandchildren.

She has 20 great grandchildren.

She has dementia.

💡 Dementia is when someone has trouble remembering things and thinking clearly.
4

She caused 2 small fires when cooking.

She forgot she was cooking.

She left a pot on the stove.

She has been skipping her medications.
